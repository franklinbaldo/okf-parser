# okf-parser 0.42.2

- adds `examples/minimal`, the smallest bundle `check` accepts as conformant,
  validated by the repository's own `okf-parser check .` run;
- drafts RFC 0009, the `fact` profile: a conservative superset of OKF v0.2
  naming its atom a fact and its scope a context, with URI types, a
  self-describing `.fact/` directory, no reserved filenames, and contexts that
  compose without flattening their identity.
