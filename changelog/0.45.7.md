# 0.45.7

- Establish a physical-materialization boundary where DuckDB acts as a transformation/query engine over canonical relations rather than a required persisted representation.
- Add SQLite as the first workload-specific target, materialized directly through DuckDB cross-database CTAS with indexes for `concept_id` and adjacency.
- Support an optional SQLite in-memory copy for query-heavy sessions without adding semantic logic or a public backend selector.
- Preserve three benchmark repetitions showing large point/adjacency speedups and conversion break-even in hundreds of hot queries at 50k concepts.
